How the decision is made

FOIR = (Existing EMI + New Loan EMI) / Monthly Income × 100

Eligible when: FOIR ≤ 50% AND Credit Score ≥ 700

EMI uses the reducing-balance formula. If interest is 0%, EMI = Loan Amount / Tenure. Invalid income or tenure is rejected instead of being silently replaced.
